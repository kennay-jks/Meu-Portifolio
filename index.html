Company History

AITO was founded in 2019 by me, Jack Kennedy Silva. At that time, I already had two years of experience in web design and brand design, but it was in 2019 that I truly specialized in brand design. That is what led me to create AITO, a company focused on building visual identities. I faced many challenges along the way and almost gave up, but, like any true Brazilian, I pushed forward and solved the problems. As a result, in 2024, I was able to hire my first employee. Today, the company relies on a team of 11 people to ensure our clients achieve results.
